  The separate native AkiTTY application remains macOS-only.

  The behavioral regression fails before the fix and passes after; an unrelated rerender does not duplicate output.

- Probe the current CLI installer in gateway checks instead of the removed 7.24.4 artifact. Failure of the current
  installer still rejects the check, and the live gateway check passed after publication.

Runtime 7.30.1 and SDK 2.6.53 remain exactly pinned and were verified from fresh upstream bytes and signatures.
