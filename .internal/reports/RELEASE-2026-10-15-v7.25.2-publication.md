## Bug review and fixes

Muse CLI completed a bounded read-only source review. The original broader Grok
request was stopped after a bounded wait without a terminal result and was not
counted as a completed review. A focused Grok CLI retry reviewed original plan-save
and retention source plus the patch, returned a successful terminal result with
zero tool calls, independently confirmed the stranded plan-save and stale-selection
retention bugs, and assessed the corrections. Its response completed after source
freeze but before publication. Signed review text and disposition records accompany
the release; tracked copies are in `.internal/reviews/release-7.25.2/`.

Four confirmed behavior corrections are included:

- Restart a plan-save drain when its final edit arrives before active-promise
  cleanup, keeping joined callers waiting for the restarted write.
- Preserve the newest queued history navigation, including a parameter-free return
  to chat; an older route cannot reopen settings or replace the newest tab/URL.
- Read the current session after retention loading and before each action, protecting
  a session selected while candidates load or another action is pending.
- Do not record a successful daily cooldown for failed retention batches.

Grok additionally proposed immediate cleanup retry for a session protected during
a scan once the user leaves it. This was treated as a policy proposal: existing
retention intentionally protects current sessions and starts its daily cooldown
on successful zero-candidate scans as well. That policy was preserved. Reviewer
suggestions were reproduced or checked against source rather than accepted as proof.

Seven regression cases fail against the original source, including both deferred
and production synchronous selection routing. All 15 related tests pass after the
fix. The release also includes the main-branch supervision and reliability changes
since 7.25.1, as recorded in `desktop/CHANGELOG.md`.
