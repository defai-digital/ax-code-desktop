and findings resolved before publication. Reviewer suggestions are leads, not proof.

## Reproduced corrections

- Restart the plan-save queue if an edit arrives between drain completion and
  active-promise cleanup. Joined callers wait for the restarted drain.
- Queue the newest history navigation during route application, including a
  parameter-free return to chat. A superseded route cannot reopen settings or
  overwrite the latest tab/URL. Regressions cover both deferred and production
  synchronous session-selection contracts.
- Re-read the currently selected session after loading retention candidates and
  before each action. A session opened while loading or deleting another session
  is protected from subsequent archive/delete calls.
- Record a successful daily retention cooldown only when the batch has no failed
  IDs, allowing failed work to be retried without forcing cleanup.

Six initial regressions failed against the original source: one plan queue case,
two route cases and three retention cases. A further synchronous-selection router
regression covers the production contract. All 15 related tests pass after the fix.

All four Desktop manifests and the release-evidence version assertion are updated
to 7.25.2. SDK 2.6.17 and signed runtime 7.25.8 pins are retained. Application
identity, credentials, source ownership and policy budgets are unchanged.
