smoke, and both Linux native smoke targets. Windows and macOS native playback

the production player correctly honors. The playback fixture now initializes its

behavior is unchanged. The obsolete signing run was canceled before publication;
the revised source must receive fresh exact-source CI and signing qualification.

windows and the six-second playback timeout remain unchanged. Local ASAR smoke
passed all six clips at 72 decoded frames each and the reduced-motion case at
