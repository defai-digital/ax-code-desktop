## Integration and fixes

The initial inventory had only main, two unpushed commits and an uncommitted runtime/SDK update. All were preserved and integrated through an isolated release worktree. No sibling checkout was reset, stashed or cleaned.

- Session mutations retry 429 responses through the runtime reset window. Bulk commits and startup recovery send requests sequentially. Archive undo and rename share the retry handling.
- Unconfirmed bulk removals remain durable across unrelated removals, undo actions and crashes. Recovery verifies its own durable copy before removing orphaned records and checkpoints each result. A failed persistence write leaves the original record intact. Unexpected commit rejection reports failure and releases in-flight protection.
- Conflicting orphaned records are deduplicated, with delete subsuming archive; a weaker archive cannot discard a failed delete. Visible timer failures are restored and reported; unload and recovery failures remain queued.
- Retention reads live activity, descendants, selection and settings before requests and rate-limit retries. Hidden pending removals pause retention, and recovery drain notifications resume deferred runs. Unchanged inventories reuse a candidate set.
- Active sessions are filtered out of the include-archived index. Archive retention preserves its non-cascading semantics.
- Archiving an already-deleted session completes idempotently and clears the stale global row without restoring or creating an archived ghost.
