Developers value **shorter time from intent to a working, inspectable outcome**: builders need to diagnose and reproduce failures; users need predictable access, understandable approvals, and visible results. Tool counts alone deliver little value.

These are proposals, not measured improvements. The audit used a stub runtime; its findings do not establish live-model success.
