The revised recommendation is now the best fit, with a qualification: strengthen the existing AX-native and CLI-bridge paths as one task-first experience, rather than building a new provider adapter or terminal multiplexer. **Strongest remaining objection:** native CLI tool/permission semantics are the risk. The producer owner is the AX Code CLI-provider owner (`packages/ax-code/src/provider/cli/*`, `cli-language-model.ts`, `prompt.ts`), not Desktop. That owner must define permission mapping, tool provenance, process-tree termination, and the explicit one-shot contract—background jobs die at turn exit and notifications cannot arrive across turns—before persistent sessions or detached execution are gated evolutions.
