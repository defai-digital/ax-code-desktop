If those mappings diverge, a page can appear under one heading but be returned—or missed—by another group’s exact search. Current mappings align, but every regrouping must now be edited in both places.

3. **Low: orphan group headings are possible.** A heading renders for every non-empty group, but pages without a `getSettingsNavIcon()` result render `null`. A group whose pages all lack icons would show a heading with no items.
