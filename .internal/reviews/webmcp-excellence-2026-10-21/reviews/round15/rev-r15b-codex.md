- **P1 — Return remounts before ownership is released.** `desktop/packages/ui/src/apps/ElectronMiniChatApp.tsx:265–271` broadcasts `viewed: false` before requesting closure and swallows close failures. The grid immediately remounts the session; a rejected close leaves both chat surfaces active. `sessionGridPopout.ts:48–52` also remounts when messaging fails, without closing the window. Require confirmed release before remounting. Current tests inject the presence message without exercising the receiver or failed closure.

- **P1 — Replacing a popped slot bypasses exclusivity.** `desktop/packages/ui/src/components/sessionGrid/SessionGrid.tsx:324–331`: the picker remains enabled and includes the current session. Pop out A, then select A in that slot’s picker: replacement drops `poppedOut`, remounting A while its mini-chat remains open. Popping it out again creates another window. Ownership must survive replacement and removal/re-addition; slot deduplication alone cannot enforce this.

- **P2 — Closing the mini-chat while away from the grid strands its slot.** `desktop/packages/ui/src/components/sessionGrid/SessionGrid.tsx:109–120`: the close listener exists only while `SessionGrid` is mounted. Pop out A, switch tabs, close its window, then return: A remains marked popped. “Return” successfully broadcasts to no receiver and never restores it. Track closure outside the component and reconcile live windows on remount. No test covers this lifecycle.

- **P2 — Nested splits violate minimum pane dimensions.** `desktop/packages/ui/src/lib/supervision/sessionGrid.ts:253–259` reserves only 160px for each child, even when a child contains multiple panes. Source-executed probes produced **118.5px** panes in a three-column layout at 492px, and **77px** panes after moving the outer splitter. Headers and chat content become clipped. Clamp each split against its children’s recursive minimum extents; existing minimum-size tests cover presets only.

- **P2 — Arrow moves use different geometry from the displayed grid.** `desktop/packages/ui/src/lib/supervision/sessionGrid.ts:409–410` calculates neighbors at the default 1000×800 size. Rendering and button availability use actual dimensions. With independently split columns at row ratios 0.7/0.3 and a 400px height, the displayed right neighbor of slot 0 is slot 1, but the action swaps with slot 3. Pass the rendered geometry into the move operation and test resized custom layouts.

- **P2 — Pointer cancellation commits the drag.** `desktop/packages/ui/src/components/sessionGrid/SessionGrid.tsx:145–158` routes `pointercancel` through the drop handler. Cancelling after crossing another pane can therefore swap or dock sessions and persist the change. Cancellation should only clear drag state and the preview. Tests exercise `pointerup`, but not cancellation.

- **P2 — Keyboard focus stays behind after moving a session.** `desktop/packages/ui/src/components/sessionGrid/SessionGrid.tsx:224–247`: panes are keyed by index, and swapping does not transfer focus. Pressing Right twice on A swaps A right and then back, because the second press acts on the replacement session at the original position. The handle’s generic accessible name and absent move announcement further obscure this for screen-reader users. Preserve focus on the moved session and announce its destination; test successive moves and focus identity.
