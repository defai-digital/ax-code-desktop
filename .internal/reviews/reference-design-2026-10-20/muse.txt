**Top finding: the direction is sound, but two P0s assume producer contracts the evidence does not establish.** Do not approve as implementation-ready until these are gated.

**1. RS-07 invents schedule-occurrence visibility before proving the contract exists.** Existing six-task evidence uses a mocked prompt boundary; it does not prove a stable occurrence identity, pre-session queued state, or producer-reported terminal states.

Acceptance: trace one schedule end-to-end on the pinned SDK/runtime and document occurrence ID, state enum, error, timing and result link. Scenario 1 passes for 3 projects × 2 schedules, including duplicate task names and one occurrence queued before any session exists. **2. The “saved hint first” recap increment can still violate RS-02.** `lastRecap` has text/time/scope and no revision binding; freshness semantics are explicitly unresolved pending ADR-148. Showing it “immediately” beside a resume action invites users to read prose as a verified, current outcome.

Acceptance: until producer semantics are documented, label it exactly as a saved, unverified summary; show time and scope only; emit no current/stale/verified badge and never parse prose into one.
